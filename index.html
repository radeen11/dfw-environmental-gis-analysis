Independent GIS portfolio project Explore locations and reported regulatory information from the U.S. EPA Enforcement and Compliance History Online (ECHO) database.

Choose between a November 2026 snapshot of 300 TRI-associated facility records and facilities grouped by the year of their latest EPA-recorded inspection. Both views use a defined DFW-area rectangle, not official metropolitan boundaries.

Explore EPA records

300 EPA ECHO facility records with a TRI program flag, retrieved November 26, 2026. This is not a list of every industrial facility or every chemical release.

EPA is queried for facilities whose most recent recorded inspection date falls in the selected year. The year view is not a count of inspections performed that year or of facilities operating then. All matching records are requested; when many match, the map aggregates them into grid cells.

A facility appears in the year of its latest recorded inspection, not in every year it was inspected. Facilities without a reported date are excluded from this view. This is not a Census-year or operating-facility inventory.

Filters by EPA-reported positional accuracy. In the TRI snapshot, 52 records exceed 100 m and will be hidden. It does not filter by pollution severity or facility clustering.

Purple cells count matching facility locations; darker cells contain more records. Cells are approximately 10 km across and do not represent emissions or health risks.

Data limitations: Being listed in ECHO, including a TRI association, does not establish pollution, exposure, wrongdoing, or risk. Classifications may be incomplete or outdated, and mapped positions can be approximate. Check the linked EPA record before drawing conclusions.
